Terms &
Conditions

  • Please present your card in the branch before checkout to redeem.

  • If the cost of the service exceeds the redeemable value of the gift card, the card holder is required to pay the difference between the purchase price of the service and the value of the gift card.

  • The full value of any unused credit remaining on a Reed gift card must be redeemed within the validity period of the gift card.
  • Reed gift cards are non-refundable, non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Once the value of a gift card is redeemed, the card cannot be used again - unless it is a physical gift card in which case you may reload it with new balance and reuse it in the branches.

  • Upon expiry of the Reed gift card, any unused value remaining on the card will be forfeited.
  • The Reed gift card cannot be replaced, lost or corrupted for any reason.
